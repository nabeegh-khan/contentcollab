Our team, Team 22, developed ContentCollab, an application for document editing and review. Our objective is to make it easier for remote professionals and industry teams to review documents. Users upload a PDF, image, or document, share it with other users either directly or through a link (similar to Google Drive), and edits are live for everyone to see. Users can also comment and annotate the document. Our application uses React for the frontend web application, DigitalOcean Kubernetes for the container, and PostgreSQL on persistent DigitalOcean volumes for storage.
