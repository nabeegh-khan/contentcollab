## What differs from 21 Nov (stated, cause not established)
21 Nov at 50 req/s: p50 63 ms, p95 148 ms, 0 errors. Today at 50 req/s: p50 90 ms, p95 427 ms, 5 errors (0.07%), also worse.
21 Nov at 150 req/s: 149.9 ok/s, p95 175 ms, 0.06% errors, 5 replicas. Today at 150 req/s: 42.4 ok/s, 71.75% errors, nearly all client timeouts, with 5 replicas ready and node memory 72%.
Not looked into further in this run (no change was made). Known: /health answers 503 when a Postgres or Redis check misses and the Redis ping gives up after 500 ms (ARCHITECTURE D14), which the load script's target hits; the error here was timeout, not 503, so D14 alone is not shown to explain it. The load generator is a laptop on one network path, which can also cap what it sends. The API's own request log for the run window (kubectl logs piped to scripts/error-rate.mjs at 15:03Z, output not saved) showed 3 requests, since health is not logged, so it cannot separate the two. Two facts from the raw data point the same way and are stated without a conclusion: all 150 req/s failures start at 14:59:48, about 5 s after the scale to 5, and api CPU fell to 34% at 15:00:39 and 11% at 15:01:01 while that step was still running, so the pods were not receiving most of the load in its last minute.
Raw files: docs/loadtest/2026-11-25T15-01-51-324Z.csv, -failures.csv, -scaling.csv

Verdict: PARTIAL. The autoscaler did what it is meant to do (2 -> 3 -> 5 on CPU above 70%, back to 2 after the load, no pod restarts, load stopped at the end). The 150 req/s step did not serve the load (71.75% errors), unlike 21 Nov; this is reported as measured and is an open item.

Update, 25 Nov 15:50 UTC (issue #184): repeating the step found no code fault. The laptop passed 3 of 6 tries at 150 req/s, once 0% and 26.6% errors back to back; a Job inside the cluster on a node without the Gateway ran the step with 0 errors (one sample). The most likely cause is the laptop's network path, by elimination and not proven; see docs/evidence/2026-11-25-load-regression.txt for what is and is not shown. The replica and autoscaler results above stand.
